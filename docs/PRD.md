# Product requirements

## Problem

New gym-goers often stand in the gym unsure what to do, how to do it, or what to use when a machine isn't available.

## Audience

All gym-goers, with a focus on beginners. A typical user started a month ago and wants to stay fit and build a bit of muscle.

## Version 1 goals

- Setup takes about a minute.
- Every workout uses only the equipment the user has.
- It costs nothing to run.
- Real users can try it from a link and give feedback.

## In version 1

Setup, Today, guided workout, warm-ups, rest timer, suggested weights, exercise library, body map, form animations and videos, progress tracking, backups and offline use.

## Coming soon

AI photo scan, AI coach, reminders when the app is closed, cloud backup, store apps and custom form video clips.

## Out of scope

Calorie or nutrition tracking. It's sensitive for people trying to lose weight, and it isn't what the app is for.
